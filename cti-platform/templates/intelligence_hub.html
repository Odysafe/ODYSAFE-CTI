Suggested Flow

  1. Start in Intelligence Workspace when the goal is a report or analyst handoff.
  2. Promote reusable findings into Profiles & Clusters when they should survive a single case.
  3. Use STIX Graph when the relationship model or exportable STIX view matters.
  4. Consult MITRE, DeepDarkCTI, and ransomware references when you need local context.
Profiles are analyst-maintained knowledge objects. They do not imply automatic attribution, maliciousness, or independent corroboration.

Common Outputs

Use the module that matches the output you need.

  • Excel report: Intelligence Workspace.
  • Persistent profile export: Profiles & Clusters.
  • STIX JSON or visual graph: STIX Graph.
  • ATT&CK context: MITRE ATT&CK.
